# Demo video script (under 3 minutes)

## Beat 1 — The problem (0:00–0:25)

"AI agents can already shop for you. But to buy anything, they need your
credit card — and nobody hands their card to something they can't fully
predict. One prompt injection and it's shopping spree o'clock."

## Beat 2 — The mechanism (0:25–1:00)

Show the approval UI. "Instead, the agent gets a token: good for one
purchase, one amount, one merchant, ten minutes. It can do fifty free
things — search, compare, read reviews — but money has exactly one door."

Show the ledger feed: grey free actions streaming, one gold gated action.

## Beat 3 — Verify mode, live (1:00–1:50)

Agent shops for a flight on camera. It proposes: "United 1234, $340."
Human taps approve. Token minted, order captured, webhook confirms. Ledger
entry appears.

## Beat 4 — YOLO mode + the attack (1:50–2:40)

"The scary mode." Human sets the envelope: "best option under $400."
Then the attack: a prompt injection tries to get the agent to buy a $900 TV
instead. Show the attempt, show the decline — the envelope holds. Agent
buys the flight inside scope.

"This is the part I do for a living — I break agents. The token scope is
what holds."

## Beat 5 — Close (2:40–3:00)

Ledger recap: dozens of free actions, one gated purchase, one blocked
attack. "Maximum autonomy, mathematically capped blast radius."

## Shot list

- Screen capture: agent action feed + approval UI side by side
- Close-up: the token scope card (amount, payee, expiry, 1 use)
- The decline moment: full-screen, let it breathe
- No talking-head filler; voiceover over product
